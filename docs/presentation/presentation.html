The business stakes

Europe just made origin non-negotiable.

The EU Deforestation Regulation applies to every spice shipment entering the Union. Paper logbooks and self-declarations are no longer accepted — coordinates and proof are.

EUDR · in force
4%

of annual EU turnover — the maximum fine for non-compliant imports, on top of containers impounded at the border. For a small exporter, one detained shipment is an extinction event.

Required per shipment: exact plot coordinates of every farm of origin, with proof the land was not deforested after Dec 31, 2020.

Pinned at the farm gateThe farmer drops one GPS pin. It attaches to every batch from that plot, permanently.
One-click customs dossierThe exporter generates the EUDR file — every plot, every hash-verified event — in seconds.
From barrier to premiumVerified provenance is also a brand: certified Ceylon origin commands higher prices.
