The Strategic 'Why' · Regulatory Mandate

The Urgent Imperative: Mandatory EUDR Compliance

The European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) enforces strict, non-negotiable traceability rules for all agricultural imports entering European ports.

🇪🇺

1. The European Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)

EUDR legally mandates that every single container of agricultural produce entering the European Union must provide verified GPS plot coordinates and proof that the crop was not grown on deforested land after December 31, 2020.

🚨 The Port Rejection Risk: Paper certificates, self-declarations, and generalized regional stamps are legally rejected. Non-compliant shipments face immediate border impoundment, fines up to 4% of annual EU turnover, and total market exclusion.
⚠️ High Stakes: European market access depends on digital plot data
🛡️

2. How Cinnamon Trace Solves The Crisis

Cinnamon Trace was engineered from the ground up to be an EUDR compliance engine. It captures precise farm plot coordinates on mobile devices at the farm gate, immutably links them to each harvest batch, and bundles them into export-ready dossiers.

✓ The Commercial Advantage: Transforms a crippling regulatory obstacle into a premier competitive advantage. Exporters provide European buyers and customs inspectors with 1-click verified plot coordinates backed by Bitcoin mathematical proof.
✓ Pre-Cleared Customs: 100% verifiable farm-to-container compliance

Blockchain Architecture · Demystified for Business

The Blockchain Part: Demystified for Stakeholders

We deliver enterprise-grade blockchain immutability without the burden of cryptocurrency fees, wallets, or volatility.

TIER 1: INTERNAL CHAIN

Cryptographic Domino Chain

Every event (harvest, handoff, processing) computes a unique SHA-256 digital fingerprint that commits to the previous event's hash.

🔒 Any retroactive database edit shatters the chain!
➔
DAILY CONDENSER

Daily Merkle Tree

At midnight, thousands of daily event hashes are condensed into a single 32-byte Merkle Root fingerprint.

🌲 Infinite events condensed to 1 root
➔
TIER 2: PUBLIC WITNESS

Bitcoin (OpenTimestamps)

The Merkle Root is submitted to public Bitcoin calendar servers. Bitcoin acts as an unhackable global notary.

💰 $0 Crypto Gas Fees · Zero Wallets
