Why traceability?

"Ceylon cinnamon" sells. But who proves it?

1
Cinnamon is graded by origin — and origin is easy to fake
Cassia is regularly sold as true Ceylon cinnamon. The buyer has no way to tell from the bag.
2
The chain between farm and store is invisible
A harvest passes through many hands. Once mixed and relabeled, its story is lost — nobody can say which plot it came from.
3
Honest farmers don't capture the value they create
Buyers pay premiums for verified origin — but without proof, the premium never reaches the farmer.
✓
The gap: proof that travels with the product
We need origin, custody and handling — carried on the batch itself, impossible to fake, checkable by anyone.

What we built · functionality

One platform, the whole journey.

1
One app — five roles
Farmer, collector, processor (two stages), exporter. Same app, acting-as-role switching for multi-role accounts.
2
Plot-to-batch linking
Farmer pins the plot with GPS; every batch from that plot carries the coordinates.
3
Offline harvest logging
Batch numbers are generated on the phone — a harvest is logged with zero signal, QR ready instantly.
4
QR custody handovers
Sale or handoff, scan-to-transfer, accepted in the receiver's inbox — custody never moves without a record.
5
Processing with yield tracking
Drying/grinding weights recorded; stage tags (/P1, /P2) attach to the number; renaming allowed — origin link never breaks.
6
Export lots
Many batches merge into one container, one QR — the buyer sees every farm inside.
7
Public verification
Anyone scans the QR: origin map, custody timeline, tamper verdict — in one second, no app needed.
✓
Tamper-evident by design
Every event is hash-chained; nightly Bitcoin anchoring makes history unrewriteable.
How custody flows

Five hands, one unbroken thread.

1Farmer
  • Pins the plot, logs the harvest
  • Number minted on-device
2Collector
  • Buys and carries
  • Number unchanged
3Processors
  • Peeling · quilling · grinding
  • Yield + stage tags recorded
4Exporter
  • Merges batches into a lot
  • Shipment details attached
5Buyer
  • Scans the package QR
  • Full story in one second

Between every pair of hands: a QR scan + acceptance on both sides. Each event is sealed into the chain the moment it happens.
